Study methods that work

Active recall

Read for 10 minutes. Close the book. Write everything you remember. Check what you missed. This beats reading again and again.

Spaced revision

Revise a topic after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days and 21 days. Short, repeated revision stays in memory longer than one long session.

25 and 5 focus blocks

Study for 25 minutes with your phone away. Rest for 5 minutes. After four blocks, rest for 15 to 20 minutes.

Mistake notebook

Write each mistake, the correct method and why you slipped. Read the notebook before the exam. Your own mistakes are your best revision notes.

Explain it simply

Explain the topic out loud as if teaching a younger child. Where you get stuck is where you need more study.

Practice before the exam

Solve old papers within a time limit. Then mark yourself honestly and note the weak topics.
