Prompt starters for AI tools

Use these with the AI tutor or any AI tool. Replace the words in [brackets]. Always check the output yourself before using it with students, and never paste student names or private details.

Create 10 practice questions on [topic] for [class], from easy to hard. Give the answers and a one-line method for each. Syllabus board: [board].
Explain [topic] to a [class] student in simple English using one everyday example. Then list 3 common mistakes students make and how to correct each.
Write a 5-question quick quiz on [topic] with 4 options each. Mark the correct answer and give a short reason why each wrong option is tempting.
Make a 40-minute lesson plan on [topic] for [class] with a hook, explanation, guided practice, independent practice and an exit question.

Quick checks for understanding

Exit ticket

In the last 3 minutes, students write the answer to one question on a slip. Sort the slips into got it, nearly, not yet.

Hinge question

Ask one multiple-choice question where each wrong option shows a common mistake. Students show A, B, C or D. Their answers tell you who to help.

Think, pair, share

Students think alone for 1 minute, discuss with a partner for 2 minutes, then share. More students speak and mistakes surface early.

Wait time

After you ask a question, wait 5 seconds before choosing someone. Answers become longer and more students try.
