Privacy Policy and Terms

Last updated: 27 October 2026

StudyBridge gives study help to students and teachers: subject explanations, study habits and exam-stress tips. This page explains, in plain words, what information we handle and the rules for using StudyBridge. By using the site, you agree to this page.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

StudyBridge is a small education service run by an individual in India. Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 86884 61707 or by email at 1234devprasad@gmail.com.

2. What information we receive

  • Messages you send us. When you use the "Ask your doubt" form or "Book a free session" form, your phone opens WhatsApp with a message ready to send. We only receive what you choose to send. The website itself does not store these forms.
  • Your WhatsApp number and chat. If you message us, we can see your number, name and messages, and we keep the chat so we can reply and continue your sessions.
  • Payment details. If you pay by UPI, we see the amount, date, your UPI name and the screenshot you send. We never see or ask for your UPI PIN, OTP, card number or bank password.
  • AI tutor questions. The AI tutor is run with Google's AI tools. What you type there is processed by Google under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Please don't type personal details.
  • Basic website data. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services may receive technical data such as your IP address when you open the site. We do not run our own advertising or tracking tools.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and run your sessions.
  • To confirm payments and keep simple records of who has paid.
  • To improve our lessons and answers.

We do not sell your information. We share it only when the law requires, or with the services listed above that are needed to run StudyBridge (WhatsApp, Google, GitHub, your UPI app and bank).

4. Students under 18

If you are under 18, please get a parent or guardian's permission before you use StudyBridge, book a session or pay. A parent or guardian should make the payment. We keep conversations with students about studies only. If a parent asks us to delete a child's chat or stop contact, we will do so, as far as we can.

5. Keeping your information

We keep chats and payment records only as long as they are needed for your sessions and for basic accounting, and then delete or stop using them. You can ask us to delete your chat at any time (see section 9).

6. Please don't share private details

Don't send us or the AI tutor your home address, school ID, Aadhaar, bank or card details, passwords, or private photos. Send only the question you need help with.

7. Terms of use

  • Study guidance only. StudyBridge is not a school, a counsellor, a doctor or a substitute for your teacher. We do not promise marks, ranks or results.
  • AI can make mistakes. Answers from the AI tutor may be wrong. Check important facts with your teacher or textbook.
  • Respect. Be polite and use StudyBridge only for learning. We may stop a session or contact if someone is abusive or unsafe.
  • Our content. Lessons and text on this site belong to StudyBridge. You may use them for your own study. Please don't copy and sell them.
  • Changes. Prices and features may change. We will update this page when they do.

If you feel unsafe or hopeless, please talk to someone now. In India, Tele-MANAS offers free, 24x7 support on 14416. StudyBridge cannot give emergency or medical help.

8. Payments and refunds

  • The first session is free.
  • The Student plan is ₹50 per month and the Teacher plan is ₹100 per month, paid by UPI to the UPI ID shown on the Plans section of our site.
  • If you pay and we cannot give you the sessions you paid for, we will refund the amount on request. Please message us on WhatsApp with your payment screenshot.
  • Payments are made to a personal UPI account. Please check the name and amount before you pay.

9. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or stop contacting you, by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing us. We will reply as soon as we can.

10. Changes and law

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. This page is governed by the laws of India.
